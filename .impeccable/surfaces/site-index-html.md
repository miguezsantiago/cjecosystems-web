# Home (site/index.html)

Scope: home page of cjecosystems.com, ES primary + EN. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: founders/SMEs (clients) and investors. Primary action: write / book a conversation (contact form + hi@cjecosystems.com). Secondary: understand the model, then Services, Projects, Team.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS on Netlify; code-led build by necessity (2026-09-24: Higgsfield refused generation on the free plan with 0 credits, so no comp round was possible; the project's standing buildPath "comp" preference is kept for future surfaces); no image generation available, so imagery is licensed stock (Pexels) plus extracted legacy assets; Formspree form `meaqplno`. Internal pages come later, so nav anchors to home sections for now.
Must avoid: AI/sci-fi clichés (robots, neural nets, particles, glow). No other firm named in claims.
Unresolved: CPC section (video, concept undefined), "Laboraz", hero video (vertical-only stock found; photo for now), high-res project renders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight: a large-firm consulting homepage (EY/McKinsey/Globant/Consultatio craft bar) that makes one young firm read as institutional. It refuses the one-page startup scroll with pastel cards and gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Brand-pinned (Conjunction-Ecosystems-Web-Brand-Design.md, 2026-09-29): Ivory Linen #F3F0E9 ground everywhere, Graphite #2B2B2E text, Antique Gold #A87C3F for headlines and large key phrases only, Forest #3F6B52 and Old Rose #B5566F alternating as accents (Rose ink #A34A62 for small text, AA). Archivo. Open two-tone C ring mark. Cards ivory with a 1px #DCD5C6 border and 12px radius, with a 2px Forest border as the active state. Outline pills, solid circular icon badges, 1.5px line icons, hairline dividers, 40x3 graphite rule under headlines, "0N / SECTION" eyebrows. No pure black or white and no dark fills; 60/30/10 composition. Bright, slightly warmed CDMX photography with rounded corners.

STORY: A visitor understands that CJE is strategy + capital + incubation for companies being built, delivered by a senior partner with AI and an on-demand specialist network, paid through a fee plus shared equity. They believe it through real partners, a real network and real projects, then book a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky ivory header: C-ring lockup, nav, ES/EN pill and a Forest pill CTA. The stage fills the viewport with the eyebrow "Consultoría · Capital · Incubación" and a gold display H1 in Archivo expanded (wdth 112, ~8vw): "Consultoría / estratégica [video window] para / la era de la IA." The pill-shaped window plays a daytime drone video of Reforma and the Ángel de la Independencia. Scrolling expands the window (clip-path) to full bleed while the type fades. Over the video, in ivory: "El rigor de una firma grande, a la velocidad de un founder.", the lede, the Forest CTA and a text link. Mobile and reduced-motion get a static layout: headline, then a rounded video card with the overlay.

FORM: Category standard (canon) chosen by the user over the roll, re-skinned 2026-09-29 to the pinned brand system, then given Globant-level impact at the user's request (v3, approved 2026-09-29: "me encantó") through scale, video and scroll scenes, never dark fills; references EY, Globant, Consultatio and McKinsey. Seed key 2e75837c. Signature interaction: the header turns from transparent to white on scroll, and the hero photo settles with a slow scale-in while the headline lines rise in sequence (reduced motion: static).
